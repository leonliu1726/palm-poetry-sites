vi.

行者The Wayfarer

遠望珠峰，俯察群山；
反躬回首，我心依然。

Gazing afar to the snow-peaks, looking down upon the ranges; turning to face myself again — my heart remains as it was.

文刃

一些行路中的照片，記下曾經停留的地方。山水與城市、相遇與獨處，都是寫作的背景；照片不代替詩，也不替旅程下結論。

Photographs from places where the writer has paused along the way. Landscapes, cities, company and solitude form a background to the writing, without summing up the journey.

vii.

音像Media

詩，本可吟、可誦、可觀。這裡彙集《千山獨行》的朗誦、訪談與詩影——以經典場景為引，讓文字落回聲音與畫面。

Poetry can be chanted, recited, and watched. Gathered here are recitations, interviews and poem-films around the collection — letting the words return to sound and image.

vii.

掌心The Palm Poetry Society

掌上有溫，心中有誠。來去隨緣，談笑無拘。

文刃是掌心詩社的發起人之一併任社長。掌心是一個真誠、自由、獨立、開放的公益詩詞藝術社群，以詩會友、薪火相傳，向全世界的詩人、作家與讀書人敞開；結集出版詩刊《螢火》，並設沙龍、學園與論壇，與各地詩友切磋交流。掌心現已另立專屬網站，內容更為完整。

Wén Rèn is a co-founder and serves as President of The Palm Poetry Society. The Palm is an open, public-spirited community of poetry and the arts — sincere, free and independent — welcoming poets, writers and lovers of letters the world over. It publishes the anthology Fireflies and runs a salon, an academy and a forum. The Palm now has its own dedicated website.
