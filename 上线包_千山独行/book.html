作品The Book

本頁介紹已出版的中文詩文集；英文選譯可在作品選讀中閱讀。This page presents the Chinese-language collection. Selected English translations are available in the reading section.

全書五百餘頁，分古詩《千山獨行》、古詩《國風入懷》、文選《南行》三部分；自十五年間近千首作品中精選兩百餘首古詩、近百篇散文。書名取自作者詞作《水調歌頭·黃州快哉亭》「千年未遠，大道欲隱士獨行」。

臺灣中華書局 · 編者推薦Editor’s Recommendation · Zhonghua, Taipei

《千山獨行》是加拿大華裔詩人、作家文刃的第一部作品，也是他旅歷人生最精華階段的創作精華輯。內容包含《千山獨行》《國風入懷》兩部古詩，以及一部文集《南行》——看作者如何在中國故土及歐美生活中感悟、吟詠，或喜悅、或悲痛、或思想昇華，將生命淬鍊成詩。第一部《千山獨行》，品味「仁者的吟詠」；第二部《國風入懷》，緬懷中土千年「士風氣節」；第三部《南行》可為致青年的「啟蒙文」。文刃以其樸實真摯不花巧的寫作手法，乘載深厚的思想內涵，同時獲得更廣泛的共鳴！

The first book by the Chinese-Canadian poet and writer Wén Rèn — the distilled work of his richest years. It gathers two poetry collections, Beyond a Thousand Mountains and Airs of the States Within, and the prose collection Journey South: the author sensing and singing his way through his Chinese homeland and life in Europe and America, refining a life into verse. Part one tastes “the song of the benevolent”; part two recalls a thousand years of scholarly integrity; part three reads as an “enlightenment” for the young. In plain, sincere, unadorned prose, he carries a deep inner world — and finds a wide echo.

許之遠先生贈詩與手迹A poem and calligraphy from Hsu Chih-yuan
許之遠先生贈詩　乙巳夏　時年九十A Poem Composed and Brushed for the Author · Hsu Chih-yuan, in his ninetieth year

許之遠先生，前中華民國立法委員，曾任中華民國教育部文藝創作獎古典詩詞與書法評審。《千山獨行》的序文與封面書名皆出其手；二〇二四年八月新書發布會，先生以九十高齡親臨。乙巳（二〇二五）夏，又自撰七絕一首，親筆書以相贈——這是傳統中國文化知識分子才華巧思，深情託付的經典風範。

Hsu Chih-yuan — former member of the Legislative Yuan of the Republic of China, and a juror in classical poetry and calligraphy for the Ministry of Education’s national literary awards — wrote the preface to Beyond a Thousand Mountains and brushed the title on its cover, and came to the Toronto launch in August 2024 in his ninetieth year. In the summer of 2025 he composed a quatrain for the author and brushed it himself. This is a classic expression of the traditional Chinese scholar-intellectual: cultivated ingenuity joined to a deeply felt act of trust.

塵世苦辛未許違，扶輪策杖掩重扉。
河山所歷無情水，只有劉郎識路歸。

The bitterness of this world does not permit escape. / Leaning on a wheelchair, propped on a cane, I have closed the double doors. / Of all the rivers and mountains you have passed, the water was without feeling — and only Liu knows the road home.
上款「劉東吾兄正腕」　乙巳夏間一揮　許之遠撰書　時年九十

首句先定全詩的倫理立場：「塵世苦辛」不是退避人間的理由；「未許違」意謂雖知世途艱苦，仍不肯背離應盡的責任。第二句寫的是他自己：扶著輪椅，拄著杖，把重門掩上——一位九十歲的人說自己已不再出門，七字之中無一字自傷。第三句轉出去，寫的是受贈者：走遍河山，所見是無情的流水。末句用劉禹錫「前度劉郎今又來」之典，而受贈者正姓劉——典故與姓氏疊在一處。他闔上門扉，路卻未曾中斷；「識路歸」三字，由此成為留給後來者的一份精神囑託。

The first line establishes the poem’s ethical ground: the suffering of the human world is not a reason to withdraw from it. “Does not permit escape” means that hardship does not release one from obligation. The second line is about himself: leaning on a wheelchair, propped on a cane, he has closed the double doors — a man of ninety saying he no longer goes out, and not one of the seven characters pities itself. The third turns outward to the one who receives the poem: of all the rivers and mountains you have crossed, the water was without feeling. The last line borrows Liu Yuxi’s “Liu of former days returns again” — and the recipient is himself surnamed Liu, allusion and surname laid one upon the other. He closes the doors, but the road does not end; the three characters 識路歸—“to know the road home”—become a spiritual charge entrusted to those who follow.

iii.

發布Book Launch

二〇二四年八月二十五日，《千山獨行》新書發布會暨「掌心詩詞藝術文化沙龍」原創作品展，在多倫多 North York Central 禮堂隆重舉行。九十高齡的許之遠先生作序並題寫書名；現場朗誦《廬山謠》、訪談對話，掌心同仁的詩、書、畫與新詩刊《螢火》一併展出，群賢畢至。紅楓影視全程錄製。

On August 25, 2024, the launch of Beyond a Thousand Mountains — with the Palm Poetry Society Salon’s exhibition of original work — was held at the North York Central Auditorium in Toronto. Mr. Xu Zhiyuan, then ninety, wrote the preface and brushed the title; poems were recited, interviews held, and the members’ work and the journal Fireflies shown. Red Maple Movies filmed the event.
