## 两处译法已按你的意思定了

1. **「管理员」→ Cared for by 飞鸿 · 文刃**（原拟 Stewards，已改）
   你要服务者的口吻，这个方向对。但 **server 在英文里是餐厅服务生或电脑服务器**，两个意思都不能用。改成 **cared for by**——站上原本就有一句 "The works section is cared for by our member 晓鸣"，用同一个说法，全站口吻一致，而且它是动词，读起来是"在照看"，不是一个头衔。
2. **「我的一方天地」→ A Space of My Own**
   你写的是 A special Space of My Own。我把 special 去掉了：英文里 "a space of my own" 本身就带着那份独有，加 special 反而把它说浅了，像在强调"这块很特别"。要加回去说一声。
3. 《萤火》《千山独行》沿用站上已有的 *Fireflies* 与 *Walking Alone Through Ten Thousand Mountains*，未另起译名。

# 第二轮：旧英文润色（同日）

- made and curated by our member ~~Tiaodeng Kanjian~~ → **挑灯看剑**

## 去翻译腔

| 原 | 改 |
|---|---|
| devoted to deep exchange and to fostering creation | given to close exchange and to encouraging one another to write |
| To broaden the reach of Chinese culture and the high art of poetry | To carry Chinese culture and the art of poetry further |
| kindred spirits **at home and abroad** in a society and learning community | kindred spirits **from China and across the world** into a society and a place of learning |

> "at home and abroad" 是「海内外」的直译，英文读者读不出那层意思，只会觉得是套话。

## 被动句改成「我们」

站上写得最好的两段（使命、萤火）都是用 we 说话的；但另外几段却是机构口吻的被动式。统一过来：

| 原 | 改 |
|---|---|
| new events are announced separately | we announce new events separately |
| Display and credit will be agreed with you | We will agree with you on how it appears and how you are credited |
| Publication and permission are confirmed with the author | We confirm with the author before anything is published |
| Once **the team** confirms your details | Once **our editors** have confirmed your details |
| write to the **administrators** | write to the **members who look after the site** |

最后一条在 index 和公约页各一处，都改了——和「管理员→cared for by」是同一个口吻。

## 查过但没动的

**公约页（covenant.html）十个小标题本来就有英文**，而且写得齐整：Your work stays yours / The licence you give / Keep to the poem / What we can and cannot do……我差点重写一遍，脚本的重复校验挡住了。那一页的双语是这个站上做得最好的，不需要动。
