Origin & Mission

「苦读于华夏，
生活在西方，
感受自由思想与独立人格之魅力——
吾辈何许人也。」

“Schooled hard in China,
living in the West,
awake to the allure of free thought and an independent spirit —
who, then, are we?”

掌心诗社缘起于二〇一七年加拿大多伦多的一个诗词文学艺术创作群，采小群邀请制度，以深度交流和促进创作为目的；多年来以自由随缘的方式举办多次诗词酒会，共同提高诗艺。为扩大中华文化与高雅诗词艺术的影响力，二〇二二年七月十七日矜园聚会时，先期入会的十二位会员以共同发起人的名义正式成立「掌心诗社」，由文刃担任社长。

使命Our Mission

在崇尚名利的现实世界中坚守高雅的精神追求，在粗鄙喧嚣的国际语境中保持礼仪之邦的风范，让中华优秀经典文化以开放兼容的心态、在吸收全世界文明成果的基础上传承与发展。

真诚 · 友善以真和善为根，守护这一方净土。
独立 · 自由独立之精神，自由之思想，不慕当世名利。
面向世界 · 面向未来海纳百川，与世界各地诗人、作家、艺术家切磋；为大众创作，划破语言边界，开拓诗意的栖居。

掌心诗社简介

倚天照海花无数，流水高山心自知；
文墨丹青千古事，临风桃李正当时！

这诗就包含了掌心诗社最早的十位诗人的名字，道出了诗社独特的开始。

掌心诗社（The Palm Poetry Society），缘起于2017年加拿大多伦多的一个诗词文学艺术创作群“掌心”，诗社采取小群严格的邀请制度，以深度交流和促进创作为目的，目前有十七名成员。他们是：文刃、风动、古土、海边、五月花、丹丹、墨望山、杨青、湖边、枫叶正红、陶然、Tim、挑灯看剑、晓鸣、石甫、王雪君、艳子。

几年来，掌心群以自由随缘促进诗词交流的方式举办了多次诗词酒会，共同提高诗艺。为了逐步扩大优秀中华文化和高雅诗词艺术的影响力，2022年7月17日矜园聚会时，先期入会的十二位会员以共同发起人的名义成立了“掌心诗社”。

掌心诗社旨在深度交流诗词及艺术心得，促进创作。通过举办文化艺术跨界的交流活动，广交诗词、艺术文化界高手，加强与世界各地诗社和艺术家的交流合作，扩大高雅诗词中华文化在海外的影响。

About The Palm Poetry Club

Leaning on heaven, lighting the sea — flowers past counting;
running water, high mountain — the heart alone knows.
Ink and brush are the business of a thousand years;
peach and plum stand in the wind, and the hour is now.

The four lines above carry, hidden in them, the names of the ten earliest poets of The Palm — a fitting beginning for an unusual society.

The Palm Poetry Club grew out of “The Palm,” a small circle for poetry, literature and the arts formed in Toronto, Canada, in 2017. Membership was by strict invitation; the purpose was close exchange and the encouragement of real work. Its early roster numbered seventeen.

For several years the circle met freely and often — evenings of verse and wine at which everyone’s craft improved. To widen the reach of Chinese culture and of poetry as a high art, on 17 July 2022, at a gathering at Jinyuan, the twelve earliest members founded The Palm Poetry Club as its co-founders.

The Club exists for serious exchange in poetry and the arts, and for the making of new work: gatherings that cross between disciplines, friendship with accomplished poets and artists, and collaboration with poetry societies and artists around the world.

《萤火》古典诗词卷首语陶然 撰

“詩言志，歌永言”。

昔在中州，各秉術業專攻之志，今來殊境，同聆風華萬象之聲。

惺惺於河海初遇，耿耿乎家國遠懷。於是掌心結社，共襄詩舉。

心遊漢唐，偶有呼盃扣舷之聚，砥章礪句之爭，故嵌名得律曰：

酒膽生風十二癡，拍磚豈礙解仙詩。
宋人未得今人句，今苑尤歌宋苑詞。
獨此丹泉澆趙土，悠然熒火起文辭。
寒梅應共靑楓俏，湖海春山又一枝。

—— 陶然

Preface to the Classical Verse — by Tao Ran

“Poetry speaks what the heart is set upon; song draws out the words.”

Once, in the Central Plain, each of us held to his own calling. Now, in another country, we listen together to the sound of a world in flower.

We met by river and sea and knew one another at once; we carry the far thought of home. And so The Palm was formed, and we raised our verses together.

Our minds wander through Han and Tang. Now and then we call for the cup and beat the gunwale; now and then we quarrel over the polishing of a line. Hence this regulated poem, with our names set into it:

Wine-courage raises a wind — twelve fools;
a thrown brick will not spoil an immortal’s poem.
The Song never had our lines,
yet our garden still sings the songs of Song.
This one cinnabar spring waters the soil of Zhao;
unhurried, fireflies rise into words.
Winter plum should stand handsome beside green maple —
lake, sea, spring mountain: one more branch in flower.

— Tao Ran

《萤火》现代诗卷首语风动 撰

当今诗坛，原创诗歌如雨后春笋，遍地开花。掌心诗社十七位成员在三四年的诗歌交流中，留下了无数美好的诗篇。他们的作品主题辽阔、不拘一格；风格独立，各领风骚。为了对过去诗路上风雨同舟的历程有个回望与总结，我们精心挑选了诗社十七位诗人有代表性的作品编入此集。另外增加了几位颇具特色的非诗社成员的作品。这样更加平衡了诗歌的丰度、覆盖面与代表性。也让大家对当代北美地区华文诗歌创作的现状有个基本的了解。每个作品都袒露了作者真善美的诗心，好与否，留待读者的慧眼去评断。

—— 风动

Preface to the Modern Verse — by Feng Dong

Original poetry today springs up everywhere, like bamboo after rain. Over three or four years of exchange, the seventeen members of The Palm have left behind a great many fine poems — broad in subject, bound to no single school, each voice independent and distinct. Wanting to look back on a road travelled together through wind and rain, we have chosen representative work by all seventeen for this collection, and added poems by several distinctive writers from outside the club, so that the book might be richer, wider, more fairly representative — and so that a reader might see something of what Chinese-language poetry in North America looks like now. Every poem here lays open the author’s honest heart. Whether it is good, we leave to the reader’s discerning eye.

— Feng Dong

《萤火》纪要古土 撰

二零二二年七月十七日，岁次壬寅六月十九，掌心群假文刃诞辰吉日聚于衿园。盛馔佳肴，豪饮畅谈，笑声哗语，四邻皆惊。席间，文刃讲述当代诗人尤其海外诗人的独特位置并出示其收藏多年之宝剑，叹曰：吾等苦读于华夏，生活在西方，感受独立精神自由思想之魅力，吾辈何许人也？当代文学使命与社会担当，舍我辈其谁也！只见星光熠熠，映射霜刃，炉火熊熊，照亮赧颜，众人于心皆有戚戚然！其时天降萤火，盘旋掌心，久久不去，于是诸位纷纷发言，掌心群出于北美生于此世，难道不是为此使命而来者？！

鉴于掌心群成立已超过五年，大家彼此交流，相互砥砺，现在与当初相比，每个人的创作能力和欣赏水平已经有了很大的提高。大家同意在维持原有的高度自由的发展状态，同时互相配合进行必要的组织工作，由先期加入的十二人以共同发起人的名义成立掌心诗社并将各职责分工如下：

社长：文刃

共同主编：古土　风动

新诗编辑：风动　　古诗编辑：陶然

评论编辑：古土　墨望山

翻译总监：杨青　五月花

活动总监：丹丹　王挺

朗诵总监：海边

摄影师及美术总监：枫叶正红

秘书长：海边　湖边

衿园聚会之后，各位帮主抒怀记事，佳作井喷，其质其量非往日所能比。所有作品收入公众号之《衿园集》，此处不再例举。为记录过去几年的创作实绩，掌心诗社同时决定出版诗词专辑，名为《萤火》，作为“掌心诗社”的首张名片，这就是本书的由来了。以后诗社会不定期出版类似诗词创作成果，并在此基础上，适时缩短出版周期。

大家都赞同，各掌门心底都有久被埋没的或逐渐流逝却无能为力的人生梦想，通过每个人的自律，通过付出一些时间和热情给他人，给掌心诗社，一定可以做出不俗的事情。我们十二铁掌上合天数，有苍天佑护，大家必当全力以赴，此生当不负自己的内心，不负重任和使命！

—— 古土

A Chronicle of Fireflies — by Gu Tu

On 17 July 2022 — the nineteenth day of the sixth month of the year Renyin — The Palm gathered at Jinyuan on the auspicious occasion of Wén Rèn’s birthday. There was a fine table, hard drinking and loud talk, and the neighbours were startled by the laughter. In the middle of it Wén Rèn spoke of the singular position of the poet today, and of the overseas poet above all, and brought out a sword he had kept for many years. He said: we studied hard in China and we live in the West; we have felt the pull of an independent spirit and a free mind. Who, then, are we? If the literary task of our age and its burden do not fall to us, to whom do they fall? Starlight caught the frost-white blade, the fire blazed and lit our reddened faces, and every heart there was moved. Just then fireflies came down and circled over the palm and would not leave. One after another we spoke: The Palm was born in North America and into this age — was it not for exactly this that we came?

The group had by then been together more than five years. We had exchanged work and sharpened one another, and everyone’s power to write and to judge stood far above where it began. It was agreed that we would keep the same wide freedom as before while doing the organizing that was needed, and that the twelve who had joined earliest would found The Palm Poetry Club as its co-founders, with duties divided as follows:

President: Wén Rèn

Co-Editors-in-Chief: Gu Tu · Feng Dong

Editor, Modern Poetry: Feng Dong　　Editor, Classical Poetry: Tao Ran

Editors, Criticism: Gu Tu · Mo Wangshan

Directors of Translation: Yang Qing · Wu Yuehua

Directors of Events: Dan Dan · Wang Ting

Director of Recitation: Hai Bian

Photographer and Art Director: Fengye Zhenghong

Secretaries-General: Hai Bian · Hu Bian

After Jinyuan the work came in a flood, better and more plentiful than anything before; it is all collected in Poems of Jinyuan and need not be listed here. To record what those years had produced, the club resolved to publish an anthology, Fireflies — the first calling card of The Palm — and that is how this book came to be. Further collections will follow, at shortening intervals.

All of us agreed: in every heart there is some long-buried dream, or one slipping away with nothing done about it. Through each person’s own discipline, and by giving a little time and warmth to others and to The Palm, we can certainly make something worth making. Twelve palms answer the number of heaven; heaven willing, we shall give it everything we have — and not fail our own hearts, nor the trust and the task laid upon us.

— Gu Tu

English Poetry is the common tongue of humankind. You are welcome here.

诗文会友，足迹遍及五洲——华沙古城、尼罗碧波、大漠驼乡、京都鸟居、印度门前，一路与各国诗人、作家、学者、艺术家往来切磋。无论你来自何方、以何种语言写作，这里都有你的位置。这面墙由大家一同填满：把你在世界各地拍下的照片，连同一句注脚或一首小诗寄来，我们陆续上墙，署名致谢。

The Palm is a small world — small enough to hold a poem in the palm of your hand and pass it into someone else’s. If you write in earnest, here is a place to show your work beside your peers; if you read serious writing, The Palm is where you will want to be. Poetry, friendship, and a flame passed on.
